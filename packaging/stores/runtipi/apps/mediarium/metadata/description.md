# Mediarium

Mediarium is an all-in-one media manager for movies and TV. Discover a title and it searches your Usenet and torrent indexers, downloads the best release with its built-in downloaders, unpacks it, names it the way you chose and puts it in your library, ready for Jellyfin, Plex or Emby.

It does the jobs of Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, SABnzbd, a torrent client and Bazarr in one app, with quality profiles, automatic upgrades, subtitles, notifications, family accounts and a built-in VPN for torrents.

## Getting started

1. Open Mediarium and follow the setup wizard to create your administrator account.
2. Your library lives in Runtipi's media folder: movies in `movies`, TV in `tv`, Mediarium's downloads in `downloads/mediarium`.
3. Add an indexer and, for Usenet, your provider. You can change everything else later under Settings.

Mediarium does not provide or host any content. You are responsible for the indexers you add and what you download.
